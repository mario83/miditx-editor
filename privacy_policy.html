Privacy Policy – Midi TX Editor

Last updated: March 2025

1. Introduction

Midi TX Editor (“the App”) is an application that lets you control the TONEX Pedal (and compatible devices) via MIDI commands, using a Bluetooth (BLE MIDI) or USB connection on Android. This privacy policy describes how the App handles data when you use it.

2. Data controller

The App is developed and published by the party that releases the application on the stores. For questions about this privacy policy, you can use the contact channels shown on the App’s store listing (developer email or website, if available).

3. Data the App does not collect

  • No account: The App does not require registration or sign-in.
  • No personal data sent to servers: The App does not send personal data (name, email, location, etc.) to external servers.
  • No usage analytics or tracking: We do not use analytics or tracking services to profile how the App is used.

4. Use of device permissions

The App requests the following permissions only for the purposes described:

PermissionPurpose
Bluetooth To search for and connect to your BLE MIDI adapter (e.g. midiA m-vave) and send MIDI commands to the TONEX Pedal. MIDI data stays between your device and the pedal.
Bluetooth Scan / Connect Required to discover and connect to the MIDI device over Bluetooth. On Android 12+, scanning is not used for location.
USB (Android only, optional) To allow MIDI connection over USB when a USB MIDI device is connected.

No data obtained through these permissions is sent to third parties or used for purposes other than local MIDI connectivity.

5. Data that stays on your device

  • Any App settings or preferences are stored only on your device (local storage).
  • MIDI commands and communication with the pedal take place locally (Bluetooth or USB); they do not pass through our or any third party’s servers for processing or storage.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If a child uses the App on your device, processing is limited to what is described above (no collection to servers).

7. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this policy in the future (e.g. for new features or legal requirements). The updated version will be marked with a new date at the top of the page. Continued use of the App after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or about data processing in the App, contact the developer using the details given on the application’s store listing (Google Play or other).
