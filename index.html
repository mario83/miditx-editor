MidiTX Editor

MIDI editor for IK Multimedia TONEX Pedal. Control your pedal from your Android phone via Bluetooth or USB.

What it does

Midi TX Editor lets you control the TONEX Pedal directly from your phone—no need to bend down to the unit during rehearsals or on stage. You can:

  • Sync preset names and settings via USB (OTG cable): load all 150 preset names and configurations from the pedal so they appear in the app.
  • Control the pedal via Bluetooth using a BLE MIDI adapter (e.g. midiA m-vave, M-VAVE MS1, or any compatible BLE MIDI dongle).
  • Switch presets (bank A/B/C) and edit parameters (amp, gate, comp, EQ, mod, delay, reverb) from the editor.
  • Save changes to the current preset and update the local cache so the app stays in sync.

How to use the app

1. (Optional) USB sync When you open the app, you can choose Start sync to sync preset names and configs from the TONEX Pedal. Connect the pedal to your phone with an OTG cable, select the device, and wait for the sync to finish. This way the editor will show preset names and let you load/save configurations. If you skip this, you can still control the pedal via Bluetooth; preset slots will appear as numbers.
2. Connect via Bluetooth Enable Bluetooth, tap Scan, and pair/connect to your BLE MIDI adapter (the adapter must be connected to the TONEX Pedal’s MIDI input). The app looks for BLE MIDI devices only (e.g. midiA m-vave). Do not select “IK Multimedia ToneX” in the list—that is the pedal’s own Bluetooth; use your separate BLE MIDI dongle.
3. Use the editor After connecting, the editor opens. At the top you see the current preset (name if synced, or slot index) and the bank (00–49) with three buttons (A, B, C). Tap a preset to switch it on the pedal. Use the Save button on the preset bar to save the current settings to the pedal and update the local cache. The tabs below let you adjust amp, gate, comp, EQ, modulation, delay, and reverb; changes are sent to the pedal in real time via MIDI.

Requirements

  • Android 6.0 or higher
  • IK Multimedia TONEX Pedal
  • For wireless control: a BLE MIDI adapter (e.g. midiA m-vave) connected to the pedal’s MIDI port
  • For USB sync: an OTG cable and a phone that supports USB host

Privacy & data

No account or registration. All communication is between your phone and the pedal (Bluetooth or USB); no personal data is sent to our servers. The app may show ads; ad providers may collect data according to their policies.
